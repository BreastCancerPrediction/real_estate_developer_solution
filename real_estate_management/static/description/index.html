Real Estate | Property Management System

Streamline your property operations with a sleek, centralized system designed for modern real estate professionals.



Overview

This app provides a user-friendly interface for customers to explore available properties for sale and rental conveniently. Users can easily place bids on desired properties directly through the app. The platform also facilitates the management of sales commissions, providing seamless control over both property sales and rentals.

Features

Property Status Dashboard: Visualize real-time property statuses — available, sold, rented — in list or kanban view.
Comprehensive Property Brochure Generation Tool to create detailed property profiles with images, descriptions, and amenities.
Commission Management: Automatically calculate and track agent commissions for both rentals and sales transactions.
Geographic Location Mapping feature for visualizing property locations and nearby amenities on an interactive map.
Installment Management: Support for partial payments and installment plans for both rental and sale agreements with due date tracking.
Automated Invoice Generation: Generate and manage rent or sale invoices directly based on lease or contract data.
CRM Integration: Manage customer inquiries and track property interest seamlessly within Odoo's CRM module.
Document Attachment Support: Upload and store related property documents such as agreements, IDs, and more.
