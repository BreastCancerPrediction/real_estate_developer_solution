Real Estate | Defect Liability Period Process



Overview

The Defect Liability Period (DLP) is a predefined time frame after the completion of a project during which the contractor remains responsible for fixing any defects or issues related to workmanship, materials, or design. It ensures that the delivered work meets the required standards and functions as expected.

Features

Categorization: Manages incident categories.
Enhanced Work Orders: Extends helpdesk tickets with work order specific fields (incident type, location, resolution, etc.).
Automatic Due Dates: Calculates due dates based on ticket priority.
DLP Case Integration: Automatically creates and links to DLP cases.
Appointment Management: Creates and links service appointments to work orders.
Problem Codes: Automatically suggests problem codes.
Dynamic Field Filtering: Filters related fields (incident description, sub-category) dynamically.
Automatic Work Order Closing: Closes work orders based on appointment status.
Helpdesk Tag Extension: Links tags to categories.
Unit Work Order Creation: Allows creating work orders directly from product units.

Action Button

Wizard that enables users to perform different actions related to appointments in a structured and user-friendly way.

Customer Availability

Wizard to record the customer's availability status during a service appointment, including check-in time and optional image upload when customer is not available.

Under Scope

This is used during appointment processing to capture whether a service or case falls under DLP (Defect Liability Period) scope or not. It allows the user to provide supporting images and automates updates and notifications based on the selection.

Schedule Appointment

This wizard enables users to reschedule or dispatch a new service appointment by selecting a new time frame and assigning resources. It updates the existing appointment with new details and creates a new record for future tracking and operational purposes.

Customer Feedback

Capture customer satisfaction feedback after a service appointment. It stores a rating, written feedback, and a digital signature, which are then saved to the corresponding appointment record.
