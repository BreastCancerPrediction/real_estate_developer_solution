Real Estate | Handover

Overview

The Real Estate | Handover module in Odoo facilitates the smooth transition of property ownership by managing the final unit handover process. It allows scheduling of handover dates, uploading of essential documents, tracking handover status, and managing checklists to ensure all necessary steps (like unit readiness, documentation, and key delivery) are completed. The module also supports capturing customer acknowledgment, making the handover process organized, transparent, and efficient for both the real estate company and the buyer.

Features

Schedule and manage unit handover dates.
Upload and track required handover documents.
Handover checklist management.
Status tracking for each handover stage.
Capture customer acknowledgment and feedback.
Automated notifications for pending tasks.
Seamless integration with Sales and CRM modules.

Automatic Opportunity Assignment

As you can see in the image, after clicking the "Convert to Opportunity" button, a wizard will open. Within this wizard, the newly created opportunity will be automatically assigned to a property consultant based on our efficient round-robin rule, ensuring fair distribution of leads.

Opportunity Details Entry

As shown in the image, once a property consultant logs in, they can view the opportunities assigned to them. Based on the customer's preferences gathered during the initial contact, the consultant will then fill in crucial details such as unit specifications, financial information, and payment terms within the opportunity.

Send Approval Request

Once the property consultant has filled in the necessary details for the opportunity, they can send an approval request to their manager.

Manager Approval and Escalation

As depicted in the image, the manager can log in and review the opportunity approval request sent by the property consultant. Upon approval, the manager can then send a further approval request to the senior manager for the final stage of authorization.

Final Approval and Quotation Creation

As shown in the image, once the senior manager approves the opportunity request, the system automatically updates the state to "Reserved" and makes a new quotation visible within the opportunity. The property consultant can then log back in and directly create a quotation for the customer based on the details and reserved property linked to this CRM opportunity.

Sales Order and Handover Initiation

As illustrated in the image, a sales order is created with the confirmed unit details for the customer. Once the sales order is generated, manually changing its state to "Sold" will make the "Handover" tab visible, allowing the next stage of the process to begin.

Initiating the Handover Process

As you can see within the "Handover" tab in the image, there is a "Create Handover" button. Clicking this button will open a wizard. By proceeding through the wizard and clicking "Next," a handover record will be automatically created, pre-filled with the relevant unit details.

HO Agent Document and QA Process

The property consultant will manually change the owner of the handover record to the HO (Handover) Agent. Upon logging in, the HO Agent will then fill in relevant documents such as the QA Snagging and QA Desnagging reports. The HO Agent will also manually change the state. Crucially, when the state is manually changed to "QA Desnagging," the ownership of the handover record will automatically change to the "Queue Team" for the next stage.

Queue Team Unit Preparation and Handover

The Queue Team will now log in, and their task is to make the unit ready for handover, as shown in the image. When the Queue Team selects a specific unit and clicks the "Submit" button, that unit is marked as ready, and a Home Orientation letter is automatically sent to the customer. Following this, the ownership of the handover record will automatically change back to the original CRM User for finalization. Following this, the ownership of the handover record will automatically change back to the original CRM User for further process.

Home Orientation Scheduling and HO Agent Tasks

The CRM User will now log in and create a meeting with the type "Home orientation appointment." Upon saving this meeting, the ownership of the handover record will automatically change to the HO Agent. The HO Agent will then log in and proceed with the handover process. This involves manually changing the state through stages: first to "Pre-CHO," then to "CHO-Snagging" and finally to "CHO-Desnagging," while also filling in the required documents at each stage. Once the state is manually set to "CHO-Desnagging," the ownership of the handover record will automatically change back to the CRM User for the further steps.


Settlement Follow-up and Finance Verification

The CRM User will now log in and manually change the state to "Settlement Follow-up." This action triggers a pop-up prompting the user to first send the NOC. After sending the NOC via the button in the first pop-up, a second pop-up will appear to send the BCC announcement first. The CRM User will send the BCC announcement, then send the NOC, upload the settlement documents, and will then be able to change the state to "Settlement Follow-up." At this point, the ownership will automatically change to the Finance User. The Finance User will then log in, upload the necessary finance documents, and manually change the state to "Finance Verified." This action will automatically change the owner to the Team Queue.

Treasury and Government Processes

The Team Queue will log in and schedule a meeting with the type "Meeting." Upon saving this meeting, the ownership will automatically change to the Treasury Team. The Treasury Team will then log in and manually change the state to "Mortgage Release" by uploading the Bank NOC document. This action will automatically change the owner to the Government Team. The Government Team will log in, upload the Title Deed document, and manually change the state to "Title Deed." Following this, the ownership will automatically change back to the CRM User for further processing.

Finalizing Handover and KHO Appointment

The CRM User will now log in and manually change the state to "Utility Transfer Completed" by uploading the ADDC document. Following this, the CRM User will schedule a meeting with the type "KHO Appointment". Upon saving this meeting, the state will automatically change to "KHO Appointment". With the state now as "KHO Appointment," a "Complete KHO Process" button will become visible, signifying the final step in the handover process.

Handover Completion

Finally, the user will click the "Complete KHO Process" button. Upon clicking this button, the state will automatically change to "Handover Complete," signifying the successful conclusion of the entire real estate handover process.
